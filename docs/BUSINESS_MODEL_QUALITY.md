# Business Model Quality

## Purpose

Business Model Quality evaluates whether available evidence suggests the company has a durable operating foundation.

## Current Evidence

RC5 uses conservative evidence from:

- Sector context
- Revenue scale
- Revenue growth
- Net margin context

## Missing Evidence

The engine explicitly marks the following as missing when unavailable:

- Segment economics
- Unit economics
- Customer retention
- Recurring revenue profile

## Interpretation

A higher Business Model Quality signal means available evidence is more supportive. It does not prove that a company has a moat or that future returns will be strong.

## Policy

The system must not infer business quality from share price, popularity, or market excitement.
