# Capital Allocation Analysis

## Purpose

Capital Allocation Analysis evaluates whether available evidence suggests the business has financial flexibility and disciplined resource use.

## Current Evidence

RC5 uses:

- Free cash flow
- Free cash flow margin
- Debt-to-equity context

## Missing Evidence

Unavailable fields remain visible, including:

- Dividend policy
- Buyback policy
- Capital expenditure discipline
- Acquisition history
- Management capital allocation commentary

## Interpretation

Supportive capital allocation evidence can improve the Business Intelligence view, but it is not a guarantee of quality or future returns.

## Policy

The engine must not praise capital allocation when only share price or short-term momentum is available.
