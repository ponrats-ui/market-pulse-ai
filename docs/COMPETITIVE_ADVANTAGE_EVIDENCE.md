# Competitive Advantage Evidence

## Purpose

Competitive Advantage Evidence evaluates whether a company has supportable signs of durable advantage.

## Evidence That May Support the Domain

Future provider integrations may support:

- Market share
- Share gain over time
- Brand strength evidence
- Switching cost indicators
- Customer retention evidence
- Cost advantage evidence
- Network effects evidence

## RC5 Boundary

RC5 does not have a verified live provider for these inputs. Therefore the domain is returned as unavailable rather than fabricated.

## Required User Interpretation

Unavailable competitive advantage evidence does not mean the company has no advantage. It means Market Pulse AI does not currently have sufficient evidence to claim one.

## Policy

The engine must never describe a company as having a moat, pricing power, loyalty, or market leadership without evidence.
