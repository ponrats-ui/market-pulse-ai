# Conflict of Interest Policy

## Purpose

This policy protects Market Pulse AI users from hidden commercial influence in scores, rankings, and AI explanations.

## Principle

Commercial relationships must never be hidden inside research output.

Thai summary: ผลประโยชน์ทางการค้าต้องไม่ส่งผลต่อคะแนนหรืออันดับโดยไม่เปิดเผย

## Implementation Rule

The current Penny Opportunity Scanner declares no sponsored, affiliate, broker, issuer-payment, or paid-placement influence.

Future sponsored educational content, if introduced, must be labeled and visually separated from algorithmic rankings.

## Prohibited Behavior

- Sponsored content affecting score.
- Commercial relationships affecting rank.
- Paid placement in rankings.
- Configuration changes made to favor a specific asset.
- Undisclosed financial interest influencing product output.

## User-Facing Disclosure

If any material financial relationship is introduced in the future, it must be disclosed where users see the affected content.

## Validation Requirement

Validation must fail if paid placement, sponsored score impact, or commercial rank impact is allowed.

## Known Limitation

The current policy documents product behavior. It does not replace legal review for future commercial partnerships.

## Future Governance Requirement

Every monetization feature must be reviewed against this policy before release.
