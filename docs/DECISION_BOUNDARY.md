# Decision Boundary

## Purpose

The Decision Boundary defines the line between educational research support and investor action.

## Principle

Market Pulse AI supports analysis. The decision remains with the user.

Thai summary: ระบบช่วยประกอบการวิเคราะห์ การตัดสินใจลงทุนยังเป็นของผู้ใช้

## Implementation Rule

User-facing analysis must use non-directive language. It may explain factors, risks, uncertainty, and what to monitor. It must not command the user to buy or sell.

## Prohibited Behavior

Avoid language such as:

- Guaranteed.
- Certain.
- Must buy.
- Sure profit.
- Risk free.
- Cannot lose.
- Buy now.
- Sell now.

## User-Facing Disclosure

Use this boundary statement where compact disclosure is needed:

> The system supports your analysis. The decision remains yours.

Thai:

> ระบบช่วยประกอบการวิเคราะห์ การตัดสินใจยังเป็นของผู้ใช้

## Validation Requirement

Algorithms must publish a decision boundary and prohibited phrases before activation.

## Known Limitation

The system can enforce product wording and tests, but users may still interpret research output subjectively.

## Future Governance Requirement

Assistant prompts, recommendation labels, and scoring explanations must be reviewed against this boundary before release.
