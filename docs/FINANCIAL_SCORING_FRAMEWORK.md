# Financial Scoring Framework

Financial Intelligence Score is a deterministic, evidence-based score from 0-100 when enough corporate financial evidence exists.

## Domains

- Financial Health
- Profitability
- Growth Quality
- Cash Flow Quality
- Balance Sheet Strength
- Efficiency
- Earnings Quality
- Accounting Quality
- Valuation Context
- Financial Risk

## Separation

Score, confidence, completeness, and risk are separate values. Missing evidence lowers completeness and confidence; it is not treated as financial weakness.
