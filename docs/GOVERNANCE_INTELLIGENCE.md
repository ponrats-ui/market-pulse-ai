# Governance Intelligence

## Purpose

Governance Intelligence evaluates whether the company has evidence of governance quality or governance risk.

## Future Evidence

Future integrations may consider:

- Board independence
- Related-party transactions
- Auditor observations
- Shareholder structure
- Voting rights
- Regulatory filings
- Restatements
- Enforcement actions

## RC5 Boundary

RC5 does not connect a governance evidence provider. Governance remains unavailable unless a future provider supplies verified data.

## Policy

Governance must never be fabricated from generic company descriptions, share price movement, or AI opinion.

## Relationship to Risk

Confirmed governance warnings should eventually become explicit risk signals and may reduce opportunity quality through a separate risk penalty layer.
