# Intellectual Honesty

## Purpose

Intellectual Honesty is the product discipline that prevents Market Pulse AI from overstating what it knows.

## Principle

Evidence before opinion. Probability over prediction. Human decides.

Thai summary: ให้หลักฐานมาก่อนความเห็น ยอมรับความไม่แน่นอน และให้ผู้ใช้เป็นผู้ตัดสินใจ

## Implementation Rule

The platform must:

- Separate facts from interpretation.
- Show missing evidence.
- Show risk and uncertainty beside opportunity.
- Explain scores without exposing proprietary implementation details.
- Keep score, confidence, and completeness as separate concepts.

## Prohibited Behavior

- Using confidence as probability of profit.
- Using completeness as investment quality.
- Using score as personal suitability.
- Hiding limitations to make output feel stronger.
- Repeating data without explaining what it means.

## User-Facing Disclosure

AI and score panels should remind users that the system is educational and evidence-based, not a guarantee or instruction.

## Validation Requirement

Tests must cover score interpretation, confidence interpretation, completeness interpretation, missing-provider behavior, and non-directive language.

## Known Limitation

Intellectual honesty cannot remove market risk. It makes uncertainty visible so the user can judge responsibly.

## Future Governance Requirement

Every new AI or scoring capability must document its evidence, uncertainty, limitations, and decision boundary.
