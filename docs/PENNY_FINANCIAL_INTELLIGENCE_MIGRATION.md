# Penny Financial Intelligence Migration

RC4 migrates Penny Opportunity scoring to consume the shared Financial Intelligence report.

## Active Weight Migration

Financial Intelligence now carries 55% of positive Penny evidence. Other domains share the remaining 45%.

## Why

Penny stocks require strong survivability discipline. Low price, liquidity, or momentum cannot override weak business evidence.

## Compatibility

Existing Penny outputs retain score breakdowns, confidence, completeness, risk penalties, missing evidence, and trust disclosure. Versions are bumped to `penny-opportunity-v2`, `penny-score-v2`, `penny-policy-v2`, and `penny-config-v2`.
