# Primary Evidence Policy

For corporate operating assets, Financial Intelligence is the primary evidence layer.

## Policy

- Target weight: 55% of positive opportunity evidence.
- Allowed range: 45%-65%.
- No other single positive domain may exceed Financial Intelligence.
- Confidence and completeness are metadata and eligibility controls, not score bonuses.
- Risk penalties remain separate from positive factor weights.

## Rationale

Strong momentum, high volume, positive news, or low price must not hide weak cash generation, negative equity, poor profitability, or fragile balance sheets.

## Governance

Any material change to this policy must be versioned, documented, and tested against expected ranking impact.
