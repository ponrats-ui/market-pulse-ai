# Why Not Explanation

Why Not explains why a candidate did not qualify for the current Top 5.

## Possible Reasons

- Disqualified by confirmed risk
- Score below minimum
- Data confidence below minimum
- Data completeness below minimum
- Provider failure
- Not present in the current scan universe

## Safety

The Why Not endpoint uses the latest snapshot and bounded exclusion index. It does not trigger a scan and does not fabricate a replacement explanation.
