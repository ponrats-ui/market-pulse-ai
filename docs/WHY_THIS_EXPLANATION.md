# Why This Explanation

Why This explains why a ranked candidate received its score and rank.

## Inputs

- Factor scores
- Factor weights
- Weighted contributions
- Risk penalties
- Missing evidence
- Data confidence
- Data completeness
- Ranking gaps

## Output

Each ranked Penny candidate exposes:

- Score breakdown
- Strongest factors
- Weakest measured factors
- Largest risk penalties
- Confidence explanation
- Completeness explanation
- Ranking explanation

The explanation is deterministic and derived from the snapshot.
